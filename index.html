02 · Our story
It started with my grandmother's clothes.

The sound of an old sewing machine, a mother who loved second-hand finds, a father who was a sculptor. Jackob reworked his grandmother's wardrobe into his first fringe jacket, and people kept asking where it came from.

“I don't need to buy something new to be a new version of myself.”Jackob Buczynski
02 · From Warsaw to Gràcia
No shortcuts.
  • 2017The brand is born in Warsaw. A former competitive swimmer trains upcycling like a sport.
  • WtórpolPartnership with Poland's largest textile sorter, and a runway show inside its sorting plant.

03 · The workshop
Take it apart.

Every piece is three layers of transformation. In the Gràcia atelier you see each one up close, then put them together yourself.

  1. 1Reworked denimA jacket that already had a life becomes the base.
  2. 2The handmade eyeBuilt from strips of recovered fabric, in the colour you choose.
  3. 3The fringeThe signature of the very first jacket, cut from recovered textiles.
